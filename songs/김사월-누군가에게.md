> To someone you are so special
영원히 잊을 수 없는
> you become a person
사람이 되기도 하고
> they can never forget
네가 사랑받기에 결국
> Because you are loved, in the end
이해 못한대도 넌 아름답지
> even if they don't understand, you are beautiful

너는 누군가에게 너무 완벽해
> To someone you are so perfect

> impossible to erase, forever

> they can never forget

> all of your unconscious and your feelings

> are singular and absolute

> And the one who wounds you

> and makes you happy

> that person too
단 하나 뿐이었다는 거
> was only ever one
하나 뿐인 사람의 사랑
> the love of the only one
내가 누구에게도
> A night when I am loved
사랑받지 못하는 밤
> by no one at all
