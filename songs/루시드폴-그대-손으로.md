> I turn my weary head toward where the wind blows
나는 쉴 곳이 없어 고달픈 내 두 다리
> I have no place to rest, these worn-out legs of mine
어루만져 주오 그대 손으로, 그대 손으로
> Soothe me with your hands, with your hands

세찬 빗줄기처럼 거센 저 물결처럼
> Like driving rain, like those raging waves

> Sweep me away, and I will lay my small body in a narrow sailboat

> If I must go now, into your arms, into your arms

> Always without a word, with your hands, with your hands
